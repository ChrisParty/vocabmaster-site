Privacy

SweetVocab for Windows · this page: 23 November 2026

The short version

  • Your words, progress and settings are stored on your computer. A copy is kept on our server, in the European Union, only if you sign in with an e-mail address — to have the same words on another computer.
  • When you press an AI button, what that feature needs passes through our server to OpenAI to write the answer. The server keeps counts, never the text.
  • No name or e-mail is needed to use the app; an e-mail address only if you want to sign in on another computer. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling — in the app and on this website.

What stays on your computer

Everything you make in SweetVocab — words, translations, memory tricks, progress, sentences, stories, badges and settings — is kept in a folder on your own computer: %APPDATA%\VocabMaster on Windows (%USERPROFILE%\SweetVocab for the version from the Microsoft Store), ~/Library/Application Support/VocabMaster on a Mac (the folder keeps the app's first name, VocabMaster). Studying, quizzes and the alphabet work without the internet. A daily backup copy is kept in the same folder.

Your plan

The first time SweetVocab starts, it makes an account for this copy of the app: a random number (like VM-7K3P9Q, shown in Settings → Your plan) and a secret key that stays on your computer. Our server keeps:

  • that number, and a one-way fingerprint of the secret key (never the key itself);
  • your plan (trial or Pro) and when it ends;
  • when the account was made and last used, the app's version and your computer's operating system (Windows or Mac);
  • counts of what you used each day and month — for example “12 new words today” — and what that cost us.

Not your name. Not your e-mail address and not your words either — unless you add them yourself, as the next two sections say.

Your e-mail address (only if you add it)

You can use SweetVocab without giving us an e-mail address. If you add one (Settings → Use on two computers), it becomes the way you sign in on another computer, so that your plan and your words are the same there.

  • We keep the address as you typed it, with your account, and a key for each device that signs in (a one-way fingerprint of it, never the key itself).
  • We send it one kind of mail: the sign-in code you ask for. The mail is sent through Cloudflare, which runs our server.
  • We use it to answer you when you write to us, and for a notice we must send about your account (a change to the terms, a problem with a payment).
  • We do not send news or offers to it.
  • A sign-in code works for 10 minutes and once. We keep only a scrambled form of it, for a day at most.

Your words on our server (only if you sign in)

When you sign in with your e-mail address, a copy of your words is kept on our server, so that every computer you sign in on shows the same. Where a computer and the account both have words, the app asks first which to keep; a copy that used Google Drive before moves only when you press Move my words to my account.

  • What is kept: your words with their meanings, translations, examples and memory tricks; how far you are with each word; your rounds (the day and how many words, for your streak and progress); your sentences; your badges; your progress with an alphabet; and for each language its name and the name you put on it.
  • Where: in the European Union, with Cloudflare.
  • What it is used for: to bring your devices up to date. Nothing else. We do not read your words, except when you ask us for help with them.
  • What is not kept there: recordings of your voice, your conversations and stories with the AI, your settings.
  • If you do not sign in, your words stay on your own computer, as before.

Sign out ends this computer's sign-in; your words stay on it. Sign out my other devices ends it everywhere else. Delete my account (Settings) deletes your e-mail address and the copy of your words on our server at once, and signs out every device; problem reports you sent are deleted too. Words on your computers stay there until you remove the app or its words. A plan you pay for is not cancelled by deleting the account: cancel it where you bought it.

Paying for Pro

Pro is sold by our online reseller Paddle.com, who takes the payment and keeps what a payment needs (your name, e-mail address and payment details) under their own privacy policy. We never see your card. They tell our server only your account number, whether your subscription is active and until when — that is all we keep about it.

The AI features

Only when you press a button that uses the AI — new words, sentences, today's story, a conversation, a new memory trick or Say it — the app sends what that feature needs to SweetVocab's server: the word or words for new cards, the words you have studied for a story, the conversation so far for Talk, the topic and level for sentences, your recording for Say it. The server checks your plan, passes the request to OpenAI to write the answer, and sends the answer back to you. The server does not store the text or the recording. OpenAI handles it under its terms for businesses that use its API.

The server runs on Cloudflare, which also keeps the account database and the stored words (both in the European Union) and sends the sign-in mail.

Your voice

Only while you hold the microphone on (🎤 in Talk, or Say it on a card) the app records up to 15 seconds. Silence is never sent. A recording with speech goes through our server to OpenAI to be written down as text; neither the app nor the server keeps the sound or a copy of it. Hearing words and replies read aloud uses your computer's own voices and sends nothing anywhere.

Reporting a problem

If you press Report a problem on a word, a story, a sentence or a reply, the text you report and the note you write are sent to our server together with your account number, so we can read it and put it right. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

Keeping trials fair

So that nobody can start endless free trials, the server counts how many new accounts come from one network on one day, and how many one computer has made. It does this with one-way fingerprints: of your internet address mixed with the date (the address itself is never stored, and the fingerprint changes every day), and of your computer's own id (never the id itself — nothing about you or your files).

Google Drive (only if you connected it before)

Before sign-in by e-mail existed, SweetVocab kept two computers in step through your own Google Drive. A copy that is connected keeps working that way until you move your words to your account (one button in Settings). It asks Google only for the drive.appdata permission: a hidden folder in your Drive that only SweetVocab can see. It cannot read, change or delete any other file. Your sign-in stays on your computer; disconnect any time in Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. We hold no copy.

Updates

A copy installed with the direct installer reads one small file when it starts (latest.json, from this website and from GitHub, where our releases are kept) to see whether a newer version exists, and downloads a new version from GitHub. Like any website, these hosts see your internet address when that happens; nothing else is sent. A copy from the Microsoft Store is updated by the Store and never reads it.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party fonts or scripts: everything on these pages comes from this site — except the Get Pro page, which loads Paddle's checkout (from cdn.paddle.com) so that you can pay; Paddle handles it under its own privacy policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host GitHub sees your internet address when you visit.

Deleting everything

Uninstall the app and delete the folder named above (uninstalling alone keeps your words, so they are there if you install it again). If you used Google Drive, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → SweetVocab (earlier: VocabMaster) → Delete hidden app data. If you added an e-mail address, Delete my account in Settings removes it and the copy of your words from our server at once. To have your plan's counts removed from our server too, write to us with your account number.
