Privacy policy

VocabMaster desktop app · last updated 2026-10-20

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in VocabMaster — words, translations, study progress, sentences, stories, settings and card pictures — is stored in database files on your own computer (on Windows in %APPDATA%\VocabMaster). The developer operates no server and receives none of this data.

AI features (optional, on your own OpenAI key)

When you use an AI feature, the app sends what that feature needs to OpenAI, directly from your computer, under your own OpenAI account and OpenAI's terms: the word or words for AI-written cards and the word doctor; your studied words for a story; the conversation so far and what you typed for Talk; the topic and level for sentences. Each request starts only when you press a button, and spending stops at the daily budget you set in Settings. The developer sees neither your key nor your requests.

Your voice (optional)

Only while you press the microphone button (🎤 Speak in Talk, or Say it on a card), the app records up to 15 seconds from your microphone. Silence is never sent. A recording with speech in it is sent to OpenAI to be written down as text, on your own key, and is not kept by the app — neither the sound nor a copy of it is stored on your computer. Reading words and replies aloud uses your computer's own voices and sends nothing anywhere.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for the drive.appdata permission only. This grants access to a hidden application folder in your own Google Drive that only VocabMaster can see; the app cannot read, change or delete any other file in your Drive. The app uses that folder solely to keep your copies of your vocabulary in step between your computers. Sign-in tokens are kept on your computer and can be revoked at any time in Settings → Disconnect, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The developer holds no copy.

Updates

When it starts, the app asks this website whether a newer version exists (it reads one small file, latest.json, from GitHub Pages). Like any website, GitHub sees your computer's internet address when that happens; nothing else is sent.

Deleting everything

Delete the folder %APPDATA%\VocabMaster (on a Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/VocabMaster) and the folder you installed the app in. If you used Google Drive sync, also open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → VocabMaster → Delete hidden app data. Your OpenAI key can be removed or replaced at platform.openai.com/api-keys.

Nothing else

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no advertising. The app does not transmit any data except as described above, and only when you use those features.
