Privacy policy

VocabMaster desktop app · last updated 2026-10-16

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in VocabMaster — words, translations, study progress, settings and card pictures — is stored in a database file on your own computer. The developer operates no server and receives none of this data.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for the drive.appdata permission only. This grants access to a hidden application folder in your own Google Drive that only VocabMaster can see; the app cannot read, change or delete any other file in your Drive. The app uses that folder solely to keep your copies of your vocabulary in step between your computers. Sign-in tokens are kept on your computer and can be revoked at any time in Settings → Disconnect, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data from your Drive removes it entirely; the developer holds no copy.

AI features (optional)

Card pictures and AI-written cards use an OpenAI API key that you provide. Requests go directly from your computer to OpenAI under your own account and OpenAI's terms; the developer sees neither your key nor your requests.

Nothing else

No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no advertising. The app does not transmit any data except to the services above, and only when you turn them on.
