VocabMaster

Spaced-repetition vocabulary trainer for the desktop.

VocabMaster is a local-first app: your words, your progress and your card pictures live in a database on your own computer. If you choose to keep two computers in step, the app mirrors that data into a hidden folder of your own Google Drive — nobody else's server is involved.

How it learns with you

  • SM-2 spaced repetition: each card gets its own schedule.
  • Flashcards and multiple-choice quizzes, memory hooks, optional AI-drawn pictures.
  • Works fully offline; the cloud is optional.
