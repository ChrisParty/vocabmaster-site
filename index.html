Questions

Do I need to be online?

Studying, quizzes, the alphabet and your progress all work without internet. New words, stories, conversations and Say it are written by the AI, so they need a connection.

Which languages can I learn?

Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Swedish, Greek, Korean, Japanese and English — each explained in any of the others. You can learn several, each with its own words and progress.

What happens after the 7 days?

Choose Pro to keep the AI features. If you don't, nothing is lost: every word you made stays yours, and reviewing, quizzes, the alphabet, your streak and your badges stay free.

How are the cards written?

When you add a word, VocabMaster's AI writes the card for your level and your languages: the meaning, a translation, how it's read, a real example with its translation, similar words and a memory trick. Every card is checked to be in the right language, and you can edit anything.

Can I use it on two computers?

Yes. Connect your own Google Drive on both and they stay up to date. Or move everything in one file.

Is there a phone app?

Not yet. VocabMaster is made for Windows and Mac computers, where a real keyboard and a big screen make learning comfortable.

Can I cancel?

Any time. Pro is a subscription you can stop whenever you like, and everything you learned stays on your computer.
